{%- comment -%} Chapter rail, derived from the story's own h2 headings. Nothing is declared per story: omit a chapter and its rail entry disappears, add one and it appears. The rail and the page cannot disagree, because one is generated from the other. Requires each h2 to carry an explicit kramdown anchor, e.g. ## The situation {#situation} The explicit anchor is what keeps a shared deep link such as /work//#situation working after the heading text is reworded.
