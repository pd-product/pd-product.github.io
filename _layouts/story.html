Inert until a story sets hero_image; diagrams are the named next content step. `hero_alt` is a SEPARATE field from `hero_caption` on purpose. alt and caption do different jobs: alt describes the image for someone who cannot see it, the caption adds context everyone reads. Feeding the caption into alt made a screen reader announce the same sentence twice, once as the image and once as the caption. Falling back to the title keeps a diagram from being unlabelled if a story sets hero_image and forgets hero_alt. Both go through `escape`: one field, one treatment. The figcaption is escaped too, which the attribute already was -- the pair used to differ. {%- endcomment -%} {%- if page.hero_image %} {%- endif %}
